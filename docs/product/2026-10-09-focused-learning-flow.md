# Focused learning flow

Today has one job: read five daily ideas and revisit followed companies. Remove the combined vocabulary/company search. Companies are searched in Stocks, news in News, saved terms in Notebook. The collapsed vocabulary library remains available for browsing.

Notebook contains saved terms, starred learning articles and personal notes. Conversations are automatically stored and reopened in Chat. Remove conversation stars and the Notebook chat category; preserve all records and redirect old links. Earlier single-answer follow-ups remain accessible from Chat. Daily selection can use recent chat topic titles without a second Notebook save; private note bodies still remain server-side, as before.

Learning details show the explanation, save and rating. Remove the redundant Explain article action, Learning source section and generic company-link exercise. The database continues to store generation prompts and source snapshots. Selection explanations and the global Chat assistant remain available.

News details keep the original report link and a compact source-excerpt note. Remove the large Source first panel and Browse more news action; the News breadcrumb is sufficient.

Next design step, not implemented in this release: one optional comprehension question immediately after a learning explanation and before rating. Saved vocabulary review belongs within Notebook Terms, with an optional short reminder on Today. A future company example must quote a real metric, company and reporting period inline; a generic stock-page link does not qualify as an example.
